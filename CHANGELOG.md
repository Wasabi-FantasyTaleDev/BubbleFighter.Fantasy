## 0.3.2 — 2026-11-13

- 2026-11-13: Pirate (horde) rooms now play the difficulty the room master picks. The room kept the difficulty it was created with, because a settings change's difficulty word was ignored, so every round played at that one level whatever was chosen. A change that carries no difficulty keeps the room's own. The first-run setup reserves room for the two new builds.
- 2026-11-13: Two experimental client builds: `tw-410` (Taiwan 2013, port 7270) and `kr-186` (Korea 2009-12-20, port 7280). `tw-410` keeps the CN2012 (`cn-324`) packet numbering and cipher; weapon-aware critical seeds, the shop supplement and lottery rewards stay off until they are checked for this build, and `tools/BFClientHook/HShieldStandIn` answers the client's HackShield calls so it does not crash after login when no HackShield update service is reachable. `kr-186` is an ancestor of CN2011 (`cn-279`) with the same ten-byte frame and cipher; its login sends the account alone, and its inventory slot, game setting, room seat, quick match, tutorial entry, start request and scoreboard row are shorter, all stated as `kr-186` differences in `packets/`.
